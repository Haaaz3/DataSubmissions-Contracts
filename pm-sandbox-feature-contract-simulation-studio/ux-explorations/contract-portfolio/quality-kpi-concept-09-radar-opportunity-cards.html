Pros / Cons / Reasoning

Pros

  • Memorable visual for executive storytelling.
  • Highlights imbalance across domains quickly.
  • Pairs a high-level chart with concrete opportunity cards.

Cons

  • Radar charts are less precise than bars/tables.
  • Needs clear scaling and labels to avoid misinterpretation.
  • Better for orientation than detailed financial analysis.

Reasoning

  • Financial leaders need to see the shape of risk, not only row data.
  • This explains why the same overall star score can hide uneven economics.
  • Use as a hero summary followed by drilldown modules.
