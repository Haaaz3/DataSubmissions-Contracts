Pros / Cons / Reasoning

Pros

  • Maps directly to settlement economics.
  • Separates realistic recovery from stretch dollars.
  • Driver cards preserve KPI specificity.

Cons

  • Waterfall categories need trusted business rules.
  • May over-index on dollars over clinical importance.
  • Requires explanation for non-finance audiences.

Reasoning

  • Pop health finance leaders think in movement from potential to settlement.
  • The current UI shows totals but not the financial bridge.
  • This is a strong executive default with drilldown underneath.
