Payor performance brief · April close

Performance is off track because utilization downside is outweighing quality-of-care gains.

The payor slice has earned $6.44M of $9.69M potential. The realistic 90-day recovery path is $1.18M, led by A1c control, ED diversion, and preventive screening closure.

What is working

$1.03M

Patient experience target tier is protected; medication adherence remains above benchmark.

What is recoverable

$1.18M

Three KPIs are within one operational sprint of their next paid threshold.

What is at risk

$740K

Readmissions and ED utilization are close to downside/gate failure.

Board-ready talking points

  • 1
    Do not treat “potential dollars” as equally reachable.
    $1.18M is near-term recoverable; $2.07M is stretch or blocked by quality gates.
  • 2
    Multiple target tiers change the intervention math.
    A1c only needs +1 point for $170K, but +6 points for full $715K. The first tier should be funded first.
  • 3
    Utilization is the economic swing factor.
    ED visits carry more downside exposure than any single quality-of-care measure.

Recommended next decision

Approve a 90-day recovery bundle focused on A1c outreach, ED diversion, screening closure, and transitions-of-care. Review settlement exposure weekly using achieved / next unlock / blocked dollars rather than star score alone.
