Pros / Cons / Reasoning

Pros

  • Explains variance across underlying contracts, not just payor averages.
  • Segmented bars make financial composition obvious.
  • Links domain gaps to specific contract-level KPI drivers.

Cons

  • More complex than a simple executive summary.
  • Requires reliable attribution of dollars to contracts and KPIs.
  • Could overwhelm if too many contracts are shown at once.

Reasoning

  • The current screen says domain variance exists but does not show who causes it.
  • Pop health leaders need to know where to deploy teams geographically/contractually.
  • Best as a drilldown below payor-domain summary.
