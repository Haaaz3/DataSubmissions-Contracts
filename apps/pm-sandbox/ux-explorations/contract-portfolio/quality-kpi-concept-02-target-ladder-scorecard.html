Pros / Cons / Reasoning

Pros

  • Best at explaining multiple targets per KPI.
  • Shows incremental value of each tier, not just total potential.
  • Easy to spot next attainable unlocks.

Cons

  • Can become vertically long with many metrics.
  • Requires careful handling of higher-is-better vs lower-is-better.
  • Less suited to portfolio-wide scanning.

Reasoning

  • The screenshot’s table collapses target complexity into one target column.
  • Financial leaders need to see tier economics before choosing interventions.
  • Use inside expanded domain cards or KPI detail panels.
