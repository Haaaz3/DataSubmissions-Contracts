Market strategy read

Scale + dollars

  • IL is the clear priority because it leads both lives and target revenue.
  • GCM and WI are comparable revenue markets but GCM has more lives.

Operational focus

  • Markets with both high lives and high revenue should receive the most complete scorecard drilldowns.
  • Low-lives markets can be monitored unless variance or risk mix is disproportionate.

Portfolio decision

  • Default executive sorting should use revenue materiality with lives as context.
  • Market owners need both population and financial accountability.

Executive interpretation

The portfolio story is not simply “where are the most lives?” It is “where do lives, target revenue, and risk terms overlap enough to change strategy?”

Use this hybrid view as the top of the scorecards experience. It tells leaders where the enterprise portfolio is concentrated, which dimensions deserve drilldown, and where a payor, market, segment, or risk-category strategy should be managed as a portfolio rather than as isolated contracts.
