Pros / Cons / Reasoning

Pros

  • Visually polished and easy to scan.
  • Consistent mini-chart pattern supports multiple KPIs per domain.
  • Target markers make gaps more obvious than numeric columns.

Cons

  • Mini-bars need tooltips/drilldown for exact thresholds.
  • Can hide absolute KPI directionality differences.
  • Less explicit about floor/target/stretch tiers.

Reasoning

  • The current design is table-heavy and visually flat.
  • Small multiples offer executive readability without losing KPI breadth.
  • Best candidate for a beautiful summary-view redesign.
