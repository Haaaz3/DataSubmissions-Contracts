Pros / Cons / Reasoning

Pros

  • Turns scorecard data into work sequencing.
  • Balances dollars with feasibility and confidence.
  • Strong fit for operating reviews and weekly huddles.

Cons

  • Depends on reliable effort/confidence estimates.
  • Less useful for explaining full contract structure.
  • May bias toward short-term unlocks over strategic improvement.

Reasoning

  • Financially minded leaders still need actionability.
  • The current view says performance is off track; this says how to recover.
  • Useful as a tab next to summary and analyst views.
